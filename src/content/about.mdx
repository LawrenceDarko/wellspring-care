# About us

We are a Christian chaplaincy and pastoral care ministry. We offer spiritual care, renewal programs, and ceremonies for weddings, funerals and memorials to people in every season of life.

## Mission and vision

We offer Christ-centered presence and care, respecting each person's tradition, pace and choices.

## How we care

- We listen first and follow your lead. You choose what to share and when.
- We never pressure forgiveness, reconciliation with people who are unsafe for you, a return to a harmful church, or public disclosure.
- We work alongside licensed and clinical care, never in place of it, and refer to licensed providers when that would serve you better.
- Our care is pastoral and spiritual. We are not a medical, mental-health or emergency provider.

## Ethics and confidentiality

What you share with us is kept confidential within our care team. We keep only what we need to care for you.

Some situations require us to act, such as a risk of serious harm to you or someone else, or the abuse of a child or vulnerable adult. If that happens, we will tell you what we are doing and why whenever we safely can.
