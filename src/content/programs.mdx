# Programs

## The Spiritual Renewal Journey

A guided program of Scripture, prayer, reflection and facilitated sessions for people who want to tend to their faith in a season of strain, loss or change.

The program is not a cure or treatment for trauma, and it is not a substitute for licensed mental-health care. If you are working with a counselor or therapist, the program can sit alongside that care.

## Formats

- **4-week renewal:** a focused month of weekly sessions and daily reflection.
- **8-week renewal:** a fuller journey at a gentler pace, with room for group and individual sessions.

Each format may be offered as a standard track (one session a week), an intensive track (two a week), a hybrid of group and individual sessions, or self-paced. Before you enroll you will see the number of sessions, the format, what is included, and any extra costs.

## What participants receive

- Weekly lessons with Scripture and devotional readings
- Facilitated group or individual sessions
- A private space for written reflections. Only you can read them unless you choose to share one with your facilitator.
- A certificate of participation on completion. It is not a professional credential.
