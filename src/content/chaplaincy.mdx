# Chaplaincy

Chaplains offer spiritual presence where life is most fragile: in hospice, hospitals, care facilities and the wider community. We serve people of every faith and none, and follow your lead about prayer, Scripture and ritual.

## Hospice and end of life

Our chaplains complement the hospice clinical team. They do not replace it. Support can include:

- Time with the patient: listening, prayer, Scripture and life review
- Support for family and caregivers
- Help preparing for a family meeting
- Funeral and memorial planning
- Bereavement follow-up after a death

Requests can come from the person themselves, a family member, an authorized representative or facility staff. We do not collect medical records or health information.

## Hospital and healthcare settings

We offer chaplaincy visits in hospitals and care facilities where we are invited and permitted. Medical questions belong with the care team; we help with the spiritual questions that often come alongside them.
