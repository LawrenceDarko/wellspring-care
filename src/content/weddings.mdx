# Weddings

We officiate Christian wedding ceremonies shaped around your faith tradition and your story, from small vow renewals to full church weddings.

## How it works

1. **Inquiry.** Tell us your date, location and the kind of ceremony you have in mind.
2. **Availability and quote.** We confirm an officiant is available and send a written quote.
3. **Agreement and deposit.** You review and accept the agreement, then pay the deposit to hold the date.
4. **Preparation.** We meet to plan the ceremony together. Premarital preparation is available as a separate service.
5. **Your day.** Your officiant leads the ceremony and helps complete the marriage license.

## Catholic and Orthodox weddings

Catholic and Orthodox churches have their own requirements for a sacramental marriage. For these ceremonies we coordinate with authorized clergy of that church and refer you to them where needed.

## Marriage license

Each state sets its own marriage license rules. We share a checklist for your location with a link to the official source, but it is not legal advice. Always confirm with the issuing office.
