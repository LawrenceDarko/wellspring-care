## What we collect

- **Contact details** you give us, such as your name, email address and phone number.
- **Request details** you choose to share when you ask for care, book a ceremony or enroll in a program.
- **Prayer requests.** The request text is stored encrypted and is seen only by our prayer team.
- **Pastoral notes** written by your care provider. They are stored encrypted and are seen only by the people caring for you.
- **Private reflections** you write in a program. They are stored encrypted and are readable only by you, not by our staff.
- **Payment records**, such as amounts and dates. Card payments are handled by our payment processor, Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number.

We do not ask for medical records or health information. Please do not send them to us.

## How we use it

We use your information to respond to you, provide the care, programs and ceremonies you ask for, send messages you request, and keep our records. We do not sell your information.

Website analytics, if we use them, record page addresses only. They never include what you type into a form or the kind of help you asked for.

## Who we share it with

- Service providers that run parts of this site for us, such as hosting, email delivery and payment processing, only as needed for that work.
- Anyone you ask us to involve.
- Others when the law requires it.

<TodoOrg title="Confidentiality limits">The situations where the law requires us to share information (for example mandatory reporting duties in the org's state), reviewed by counsel.</TodoOrg>

## How long we keep it

- General inquiries: 3 years after the inquiry is closed.
- Pastoral notes: 7 years, then deleted.
- Private reflections: deleted as soon as you ask.
- Financial records: 7 years.

## Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, and you can change which messages you receive. [Contact us](/contact) to make a request.
