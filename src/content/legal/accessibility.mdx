# Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site and take part in our services.

## Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. That includes keyboard access, visible focus, labeled form fields, readable contrast and respect for reduced-motion settings.

This site is still being built, and some pages may fall short. We test as we go and fix problems we find.

## Ask for help or another format

If something on this site does not work for you, or you would like information in another format, [contact us](/contact). Tell us the page and what happened, and we will help you another way.
