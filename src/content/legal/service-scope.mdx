# Service scope

We offer pastoral and spiritual care. This page explains what that includes and what it does not, so you can find the right help.

## What we offer

- Spiritual care and prayer, in person or online.
- Chaplaincy in hospice, healthcare and community settings.
- Pastoral support for individuals, couples and families.
- Spiritual renewal programs for groups.
- Weddings, funerals, memorials and events.

## What we do not offer

- **Emergency help.** We are not an emergency service, and messages are not monitored continuously.
- **Medical or mental-health care.** We are not a medical or mental-health provider. Our care is not psychotherapy or clinical counseling.
- **Care for minors.** Our services are for adults aged 18 or older. A parent or guardian can [contact us](/contact) about family support.

## Working with other care

Our chaplains complement hospice and healthcare teams. They do not replace them. We do not collect medical records or health information.

When licensed care would serve you better, we will help you find a referral to a licensed provider.

## Your choices

We never pressure forgiveness, reconciliation with people who are unsafe for you, a return to a harmful church, or public disclosure. You choose what to share and when.
