# Funerals and memorials

We are sorry for your loss. We can help you honor the person you love, at your pace.

## What we offer

- Funeral officiating and Christian funerals
- Memorial services and celebrations of life
- Graveside services
- Family prayer
- Help preparing a eulogy
- A planning conversation
- Bereavement support: a single conversation, or 4, 8 or 12 weeks
- Follow-up after hospice care

## Where

Online, at home, at a funeral home, church or cemetery, or somewhere else that matters to your family.

## Cost

You can talk with us before anything is decided. There is no payment to make an inquiry. If cost is a concern, tell us. We have a hardship option.
