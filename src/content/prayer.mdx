# Prayer requests

Our prayer team would be glad to pray with you and for you. You can share as much or as little as you like.

## Share a request

The online prayer request form is coming soon. Until then, you can [contact us](/contact) and let us know it is a prayer request. Please share only what you are comfortable sending in a general message.

## How requests made through the form will be handled

- **Private by default.** Your request is seen only by our prayer team.
- **Protected.** Request text is stored encrypted.
- **Your choice of follow-up.** Tell us whether you would like to hear from us, and how, or prefer no contact.
- **Not published.** We do not display prayer requests publicly.

Prayer requests are not monitored around the clock.
