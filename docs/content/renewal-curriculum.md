# Renewal curriculum (seed source)

Read only by Phase 3 curriculum tasks. Lesson bodies are drafts for pastoral review before launch.
Global notes for every lesson: optional participation; no pressure to forgive, reconcile, return to a church or disclose; not a substitute for licensed care; include the "when to seek more support" box.

## Four-week program
Default: 1 facilitated session/week, 90-120 min; optional 2nd weekly session (intensive); daily devotional 10-20 min; optional pastoral check-in; final personalized plan.

### Week 1 - Recognizing the Wound and Creating Space for Renewal
Topics: spiritual hurt and disappointment; what has affected one's relationship with God; compassionate, nonjudgmental space; biblical lament and honest prayer; personal spiritual needs; boundaries and goals.
Scripture: Psalm 34; Psalm 42; Matthew 11:28-30.
Exercises: spiritual wellness inventory; guided journaling; Scripture reflection; optional prayer and quiet.

### Week 2 - Receiving Grace and Rebuilding Trust
Topics: God's grace and compassion; disappointment, rejection, shame, unmet expectations; God's character vs harmful behavior by religious leaders; forgiveness without minimizing harm; rebuilding trust at one's own pace.
Scripture: Psalm 23; Romans 8; Ephesians 2:1-10.
Exercises: guided Scripture meditation; grace and identity reflection; optional prayer with facilitator; reflection worksheet.

### Week 3 - Renewing the Mind and Reestablishing Spiritual Practices
Topics: Scripture meditation; sustainable prayer life; spiritual triggers and reminders; boundaries and spiritual self-care; gratitude, rest, rhythms; faith without performance pressure.
Scripture: Romans 12:1-2; Philippians 4:4-9; John 15.
Exercises: breath prayer with Scripture; guided Christian meditation; prayer journaling; devotional planning; identifying supportive people and places.

### Week 4 - Moving Forward With Hope and Purpose
Topics: a healthy spiritual life; values and purpose; healthy Christian community; sustainable disciplines; when professional support may help; personalized 30-day plan.
Scripture: Isaiah 43:18-19; Colossians 3:1-17; Philippians 1:6.
Exercises: growth plan; optional commissioning prayer; growth reflection; follow-up planning.

Completion resources: digital workbook, prayer journal, Scripture meditation guide, growth plan, 30-day devotional, certificate of participation (not a credential), optional follow-up appointment.

## Eight-week program
1 facilitated session/week. Same resources plus: 2 optional pastoral check-ins, deeper weekly reflections, optional small groups, midpoint reflection (after week 4), 60-day growth plan, optional alumni group.

1. Recognizing Spiritual Wounds
2. Understanding Spiritual Hurt, Grief, and Disappointment
3. Biblical Lament, Honesty, and Reconnecting With God
4. Grace, Identity in Christ, and Freedom From Performance-Based Faith
5. Forgiveness, Boundaries, and Healthy Relationships
6. Renewing the Mind Through Scripture, Prayer, and Spiritual Practices
7. Restoring Purpose, Calling, and Healthy Community
8. Building a Sustainable Spiritual Life and Personal Renewal Plan

Scripture and exercises for weeks 1-8: reuse the four-week mappings (8-week weeks 1-2 -> 4-week week 1, 3 -> lament material, 4 -> week 2, 5 -> week 2 forgiveness + week 3 boundaries, 6 -> week 3, 7-8 -> week 4) until the organization supplies its own.

## Tracks
standard 1/week; intensive 2/week (4-week intensive = 8 sessions); hybrid 1 group + 1 optional individual/week; self-paced (content + optional scheduled support).
